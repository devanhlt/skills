## Synthesis rules

The references restate design principles in original language. They intentionally exclude original PDFs, full extracted text, and a verbatim catalogue of the book's lens cards.

Direct source ideas: essential experience; interdependent design elements; iteration and prototyping; relevant balance dimensions; feedback; audience-specific documentation; playtesting; Day's four reward lessons.

Applied extensions: response workflow; state/specification tables; probability examples; token guarantees/pity as options; migration patterns; measurement criteria; PvP counterplay application; modern accessibility checks. Cite these as analysis or proposals rather than as exact author instructions.

Resolve apparent conflicts through the project's experience goal. Schell discusses variable rewards; Day stresses choosing between certainty and randomness according to their strengths. Neither establishes that every reward must be random. Day's powerful-fantasy lesson does not negate fairness, meaningful choices, or the consequences of a dominant option.

## Lookup when originals are available

Ask the user to attach the original only when an exact passage or figure is needed and the file is unavailable. Search by chapter/topic and inspect complete relevant pages. Preserve the distinction between printed pages and viewer pages. The installed skill has no dependency on the creator's Desktop paths.
